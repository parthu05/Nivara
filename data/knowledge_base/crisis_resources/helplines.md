# Crisis resources

Nivara is not emergency care. If you are in danger, contact local emergency services immediately.

## Immediate danger
- Call your local emergency number (for example 911 in the United States, 112 in much of Europe, 999 in the United Kingdom).
- If you can, stay with someone you trust until help arrives.

## Suicide and emotional crisis
- United States: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
- International locator: https://www.iasp.info/suicidalthoughts/
- If you are supporting someone else, stay with them if it is safe and help them reach one of the numbers above.

## How Nivara should respond
When a person may be in crisis, stop ordinary coaching. Acknowledge that they matter, encourage real-world help, and share the resources above. Do not give instructions related to self-harm.
