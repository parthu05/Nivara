# Stress basics

Stress is a body-and-mind response to demand. Short bursts can be useful. Ongoing stress without rest can affect sleep, mood, and focus.

## Helpful questions
- What is one demand I can postpone or shrink today?
- Where is there 10 minutes for a break?
- Who could I tell that I am stretched?

## Gentle options
- Unclench the jaw and drop the shoulders
- Take a short walk without a phone
- Write down worries, then pick one that is actually actionable

If stress includes panic, chest pain, or thoughts of wanting to die, that is beyond self-help — seek urgent real-world support.
