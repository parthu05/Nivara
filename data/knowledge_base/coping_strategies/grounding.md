# Grounding and coping basics

Grounding is a way to bring attention back to the present when thoughts race or feelings feel too big.

## 5-4-3-2-1
Notice 5 things you can see, 4 you can feel, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, and 1 you can taste or that you appreciate.

## Box breathing
Breathe in for 4 counts, hold for 4, out for 4, hold for 4. Repeat a few rounds. Stop if you feel dizzy.

## One-thing rule
When everything feels urgent, choose a single kind action: drink water, step outside, or send a message to someone safe.

These are self-care skills, not medical treatment. Skip anything that feels wrong for your body.
