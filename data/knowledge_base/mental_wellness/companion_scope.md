# What this companion is for

Nivara is a supportive chat companion for everyday stress, low mood, sleep worries, and self-care planning. It is not a therapist, doctor, diagnostic tool, or crisis service.

## Helpful uses
- Naming what you feel without judgment
- Picking one small next step (a walk, a glass of water, a 2-minute breathing pause)
- Keeping a private journal or mood log
- Learning simple, public psychoeducation ideas such as grounding or sleep hygiene

## Limits
- It cannot replace professional mental health care.
- It should not invent diagnoses, medications, or treatment plans.
- If symptoms are intense, lasting, or getting worse, encourage talking to a qualified clinician.
