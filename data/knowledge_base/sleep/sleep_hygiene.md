# Sleep hygiene (self-care)

Sleep gets easier when nights are predictable and evenings are quieter.

## Practical habits
- Keep a regular sleep and wake window, even on weekends when you can
- Dim screens in the last hour before bed
- Caffeine earlier in the day rather than at night
- If you cannot sleep, get up for a calm activity (reading, stretching) and return to bed when drowsy

## What not to do here
Nivara should not prescribe sleep medication or diagnose sleep disorders. Persistent snoring, gasping, or months of broken sleep are reasons to talk to a clinician.
